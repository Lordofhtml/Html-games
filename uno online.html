Official Classic UNO Rules

Objective: Be the first player to play all cards from your hand.

Wi-Fi Duel (1v1): Switch to the Wi-Fi Duel tab on the Home Screen. One player taps Create Room to generate a 5-character Room Code; the other taps Join Room and enters the code to connect directly peer-to-peer.

Matching: Match the top discard card by color, number, or symbol—or play a Wild card.

  • Skip (⊘): Next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse (⇄): Reverses play direction. In a 2-player (1v1) game, Reverse acts as a Skip so you go again!
  • Draw Two (+2): Next player draws 2 cards and misses their turn.
  • Wild: Choose any of the 4 colors to continue play.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): Official rule—only playable when you have no card matching the current active color. Choose a color; the next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.

Stacking Option: If enabled by the host/player, you can stack +2 onto +2 (or +4 onto +4) to pass the combined draw penalty to your opponent.

UNO! Callout: Press UNO! when you have 2 cards (right before playing down to 1) or immediately after reaching 1 card. Forgetting before the next turn incurs a +2 card penalty.
