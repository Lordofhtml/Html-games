How to Play

1

Connect Dots: Tap any dashed line between two adjacent dots to claim that edge.

2

Complete Boxes & Bonus Turns: Drawing the 4th side of a 1×1 square captures the box for 1 point and gives you an immediate bonus turn.

3

Avoid 3-Sided Traps: Try not to draw the 3rd side of a box unless forced, or your opponent will capture it and chain adjacent boxes.

4

Win the Grid: Once all boxes are claimed, the player with the most boxes wins!
